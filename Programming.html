Mac OS - Clear the Clipboard.

As of Mac OS 15 Sequoia, Apple still has NOT implemented the ability to quickly clear the clipboard from stuff copied, and cut.

This means you can paste whatever remains on the clipboard which for me is pretty annoying.

I have made a script throughout the past fall to fix that, this will run in the apple script editor, you may also pin this to your menu bar.
